Dr. Hong Chen is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of New Brunswick (UNB), holds the Cisco Research Chair in IoT, and leads the Wireless Intelligent Networking and Computing (WINC) Lab.

Dr. Chen received the PhD degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from McMaster University in 2023, with a dissertation on resource management for mobile computation offloading.

Dr. Chen's research interests include B5G/6G networks, intelligent IoT systems, semantic communications, multi-access edge computing (MEC), joint resource allocation, edge intelligence, generative AI and large language models (LLMs), and machine learning for communications and networking. This work has appeared in venues including the *IEEE Transactions on Communications*, *IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing*, *IEEE Internet of Things Journal*, *IEEE Transactions on Green Communications and Networking*, and *IEEE Communications Letters*, and at conferences including IEEE ICC, WCNC, and PIMRC.
